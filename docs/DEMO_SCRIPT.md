# Demo script (under 2 minutes)

One story: a fire on a Moon rover, and what NASA's 24 microgravity combustion
investigations can and cannot tell us about it. Numbers below are from the current data;
re-check them on the deployed app before recording.

Before recording: open the public app in a private window, 1080p, browser zoom 110%,
sidebar habitat set to **ISS**, Scenario page weights at default.

| # | Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:15 | **Home** | "Apollo 1, Apollo 13 and Mir: all three spacecraft fires involved extra oxygen. NASA's Moon base plans to run at 30% oxygen. What does NASA's fire research tell us about that?" |
| 2 | 0:15–0:30 | **Investigations**, scroll the cards | "We pulled all 24 combustion investigations from NASA's PSI database, about 2,000 test runs, and put 1,858 of them into one standard table. Each one is summarized and tagged, and every card links its DOI." |
| 3 | 0:30–1:10 | **Sidebar → Habitat atmosphere → Moon Base / pressurized rover**, then **Scenario Explorer**, scenario pill **Electrical overheat**, tab **Smoke** | "Electrical overheating is the most likely way a spacecraft fire starts. In the smoke phase, SAME-R and SAME rank first: they measured smoke from overheated spacecraft materials such as Kapton and Teflon. The warning says it: the ISS detector often missed small particles from Teflon and Kapton." In the sidebar **Ranking weights**, move **Condition overlap** to 1 and the others to 0: "Every score is explained; weight only atmosphere match and the ranking changes, so you can see exactly why." Click **Reset weights**. |
| 4 | 1:10–1:40 | **Gap map** (habitat still Moon) | "Here is every run that reports oxygen and pressure. The Moon base star sits on the same oxygen partial pressure as the ISS, but at a higher oxygen fraction: less nitrogen to soak up heat, so materials ignite and spread more easily. 341 runs sit near ISS air; only 64 near the Moon base, and none near the spacesuit." |
| 5 | 1:40–1:50 | **Scenario Explorer**, any tab, point at the **No data yet** card | "And no PSI test was run in partial gravity. Microgravity and Earth bound it; the FM2 and LUCI experiments will fill the gap." |
| 6 | 1:50–2:00 | **Gap map**, click **Download tests.csv** | "The standardized table downloads for modellers, every claim links to a DOI, and this is a research tool, not an operational safety system." |

Optional 10 s hook (Flame Lab, before step 2): click **Same fire on Earth**, then **Fabric fire on the ISS**: "On Earth a flame is a rising teardrop; in orbit there is no buoyancy, so it becomes a dim flame fed only by the fans. Turn the fans off and it starves." Point out the label: illustrative, not a simulation.

Alternative for step 4: on the Scenario page, drag the orange point in *Design your own atmosphere* to about 9 psi and 40% O₂. The readout turns orange: **0 test runs nearby**, and the ranking updates for that atmosphere.

Backup lines if time allows (Investigations, bottom): "Earth tests are not always conservative:
PMMA flames kept spreading at 17% oxygen in microgravity but not at 18% on Earth. But it cuts
both ways: one silicone sample burned on Earth and not in low gravity. We show both."
